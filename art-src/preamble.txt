Art direction for the project "Terrarium" — keep identical across every image: a hand-painted gouache ILLUSTRATION (not a photograph, not a 3D render, not photorealistic), flat-ish matte paint with soft visible brush texture and a fine paper grain; no ink outlines, shapes are defined by colour and light; naturalist field-guide plate, calm and precise. Palette: pale celadon fog #C9D8D2, deep moss #1F3A33, lichen yellow #D6C35A, beetle violet #6B4FA0, spore cyan #5FE3D8 (glows only), peat brown #3B2E26, salmonberry pink #F07A8C. Lighting: one warm late-afternoon key light from the upper left filtered through glass, cool blue-green fill from the right, soft ambient occlusion, thin warm rim light on upper-left edges. Lifted blacks, never pure black. Original design — not in the style of any existing studio, film or website. No text, no letters, no logos, no watermark, no border, no frame.
